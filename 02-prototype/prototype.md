## Ask every stakeholder

Value is hard to set a minimum for, so any stated dollar or hour figure counts and the ranking within a tier follows the size of that figure. These questions help turn vague value into numbers and confirm readiness. They are suggestions, not stakeholder statements.

- What does this cost you or the team today, in hours per week or dollars per month, and how did you work that out?
- Where does the data live, who owns it, and has anyone confirmed we can access it?
- Who else, in which departments or brands, does this same problem affect, and would they use the same solution?
- What would success look like, and is there a date or event driving the timing?
- Does the data involved need Security, Legal or Finance sign-off before an AI tool touches it?
